# Privacy

This public repository contains source and fictional examples only. Runtime data belongs to the person who installs it. Keep CVs, contact lists, messages, approvals, screenshots of accounts, tokens, databases and exports in `data/` or `private/`, which are ignored by Git.

Git ignores do not remove already committed data. Review `git diff --cached` before pushing. Do not publish the running CRM or forward its port publicly. GitHub Codespaces has private forwarding by default; leave it private. Local data and backups are not encrypted by this application; protect the device and disk. Dependencies, cloud hosts and connected providers have their own privacy policies.

Use your own connected accounts. Do not paste credentials into an AI conversation, a README, a screenshot or a public issue. Credentials belong in your host's secret manager or your local environment. The software has no telemetry service of its own. Contacting a connected provider necessarily shares the information required for that action.
